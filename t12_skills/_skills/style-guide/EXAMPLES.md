# Style Guide Examples

## Email Subject Lines

| Before                                             | After                                   | Rule Applied                          |
|----------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------|---------------------------------------|
| IMPORTANT!!! Account update notice                 | Your account settings changed           | No all caps, no exclamation marks     |
| We are pleased to announce our Q3 newsletter       | Q3 news: three new features             | Say what the reader gets, under 40 characters |
| Re: Re: Request regarding your recent submission   | Your request is approved                | Put the outcome first                 |
| Newsletter #12                                     | Your monthly update: what's new in May  | Describe the content, not the series  |

## Email Body Rewrites

**Before:**

> We are pleased to inform you that your request has been processed successfully by our dedicated support team. Please be advised that the team will utilize the information you provided in order to facilitate the resolution of the issue at the earliest possible juncture.

**After:**

> Good news: we have fixed your request. Thanks for your patience.
>
> **View your ticket**
>
> Thanks,
> The Support Team

**Changes:** shortened 45 words to 13. Replaced "pleased to inform" and "processed successfully" with a plain result. Replaced "utilize" and "in order to" with simpler words. Added one call to action.

## Common Jargon → Plain English

| Jargon                | Plain English          |
|-----------------------|------------------------|
| leverage              | use                    |
| utilize               | use                    |
| synergy               | working together       |
| facilitate            | help, make it possible |
| at your earliest convenience | soon           |
| going forward         | from now on            |
| deep dive             | close look             |
| bandwidth             | time                   |

## Social Media Rewrites

**LinkedIn**

**Before:** We are excited to leverage our synergistic platform to facilitate enterprise transformation. #innovation #synergy #digital #enterprise

**After:** Our team cut report build time from two days to two hours. Here is how we did it. #DataEngineering

**Changes:** replaced "leverage", "synergistic", and "facilitate" with plain words. Opened with a concrete result. Cut hashtags from 4 to 1.

**Twitter/X**

**Before:** Please be advised that our new feature is now available for utilization by all users!!!

**After:** The new export feature is live for everyone. Try it today.

**Changes:** removed "please be advised" and "utilization". Removed extra exclamation marks. Led with the key fact.

## Support Reply Rewrites

**Before:**

> Your issue has been noted. Users must reset their password through the portal. Failure to do so will result in account lockout.

**After:**

> We know being locked out is frustrating, and we are here to help.
>
> To get back in:
>
> 1. Open the sign-in page and click **Forgot password**.
> 2. Enter the email you use for your account.
> 3. Follow the link in the email and set a new password.
>
> Reply if you need more help. We are happy to walk you through it.

**Changes:** added an empathy opening. Replaced the warning with a numbered procedure. Bolded UI elements. Ended with an offer of help.
