# Style Guide Skill

Use this skill to write or rewrite text in our brand voice.

## File Map

- [REFERENCE.md](REFERENCE.md): brand voice at a glance and rules for each content type. Read the section that matches the content type.
- [EXAMPLES.md](EXAMPLES.md): before and after rewrites. Use them to check your result.

## Core Principles

- **Voice:** clear, confident, and warm. We sound like a helpful expert, not a legal document.
- **Tone:** empathetic for support content, energetic but honest for marketing content.
- **Length:** short sentences, about 15 to 20 words on average. Cut filler words.
- **Person:** "we" for the company and "you" for the reader. Avoid "the customer" and "the user".

## Workflow

1. Identify the content type: email, subject line, blog post, heading, social post, or support reply.
2. Apply the core principles above.
3. Open [REFERENCE.md](REFERENCE.md) and read the rules for that content type.
4. Open [EXAMPLES.md](EXAMPLES.md) and check that your text follows the same pattern as the matching examples.
5. Return the polished text first. Then add a short **Changes:** list with the main edits and the rule each edit follows.

## Instant Rules

Check every text against this list before you return it:

- No jargon. Replace "leverage", "synergy", "utilize" with plain words such as "use".
- Prefer active voice. "We fixed the issue" instead of "The issue was fixed".
- Use contractions in support and social content, but not in formal emails.
- Spell out numbers under ten. Use digits for counts, measurements, and dates.
- Use the Oxford comma in lists.
- One exclamation mark per text at most, and only in marketing content.
